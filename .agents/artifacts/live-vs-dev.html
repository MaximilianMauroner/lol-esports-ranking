Rankings, desktop

The rail is unchanged from the version you liked. Tier density as a 2x2 grid with counts and the team names under each, Movement with riser, faller, upset and the confidence bar, then Region power. Sticky, 320px, on the right. Direction D briefly replaced it with a strip and then a compact list; both are reverted.

Above the table the two scope rows became one timeline with a progress fill on the ongoing split (C), and the compare dock sits under it announcing the feature before you use it (E).

In the row itself the score is now the only figure at --t-6 and movement sits under it rather than in its own column (A). A Form column was added: form was on every standing and the board had never shown it.

Rankings, mobile

The board is no longer a squeezed table. Each row is a card: rank, tier, crest, name, league, record, win rate, score, movement and the pick control, all at readable sizes. Live drops the record column, the sparkline and the rank pill, and shrinks type to 0.6rem to fit.

The compare dock is fixed to the bottom edge here, not inline. The board is over 4,000px long on a phone, so an inline dock scrolls out of reach the moment you start picking.

What changed, and where

DirectionFilesWhat to look at
A TeamsView, rankings.css Six columns to five, movement folded into the score cell, score at --t-6/800. New Form column. Record also appears in the team meta line, so hiding that column below 1180px no longer loses it.
B rankings.css Below 640px the table becomes a card grid. Pure CSS, no second JSX tree and no JS breakpoint.
C ScopeBar.tsx (new), App Season select plus one split track. The ongoing split's fill uses the latest rated match date, not the wall clock, so what you see matches the data the page is built from.
D RankingShowcase, TeamsView Reverted. The rail keeps the three panels you liked: Tier density, Movement, Region power. The only thing kept from D is that the board table now carries a Form column, which fills the width the row layout freed up.
E CompareDock.tsx (new), App Always present with two empty slots. At two picks it shows the score gap and win probability inline, computed by the existing estimatePublicMatchup rather than a second implementation.
radius ui.tsx Two passes. The inset child needed a 3px radius to nest inside an 8px parent, off the scale, so the inset went and the container now clips the segments. The accent ring I added with it was then clipped at the corners instead of following them, which read as a square ring in a rounded box, so the ring went too: fill and weight carry the selection. Separately the scope bar's select and split buttons did not share a top edge, because a bare select was centred against the whole track block. Both groups are now a fixed-height label over a control at --control-h, verified at 0px offset rather than by eye.

Open questions

  • The team meta line truncates on a phone. "LCK · 29-10 · 74%" becomes "LCK · 29-10 · ...". The record survives, the win rate is what gets cut. Tell me if you would rather drop the league or the win rate there.
  • Rankings is still taller on desktop, 2796px to 3193px, because the scope timeline and the compare dock both sit above the board.
  • The "Full year" control is now a small ghost link above the split track rather than a tab. It is easy to miss.
  • On mobile the rail stacks under the board again, as it did before. That is how you had it, so I left it, but it does mean the S/A/B/C badges in the rank column arrive before the tier legend explains them.
  • Direction B hides the uncertainty band (±65) on phone cards. It is still in the row tooltip and the team drawer.
