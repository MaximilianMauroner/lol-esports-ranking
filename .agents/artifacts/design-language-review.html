1. Nav and scope bar

Three rows of tabs sat on top of each other in three different shapes with two different active colours. Gold marked the active nav item while cyan marked the active season, so --rank-gold meant both "rank quality" and "you are here". Now one Button variant="tab" serves all three rows, one --selected-* treatment marks selection everywhere, and gold is reserved for rank quality.

Also. The 226 KB, 512x512 logo.png that rendered into a 36px box is replaced by the 400-byte logo.svg that was already in the repo, unused. Nav taglines are gone: they restated the label and were hidden below 1040px anyway.

Corrected after review: the split tabs

The first pass left the checkpoint tabs taller than the season tabs beside them, ragged in width, and with a "Full year / 2026" subtitle that repeated the season tab three places to its left. Both rows now share one explicit --scope-h, checkpoints share a min-w, and a divider separates the two groups.

2. Page header and the first-visit explanation

Regions opened with a clear paragraph explaining its metric. Rankings, the view every first-time visitor lands on, opened with a filter bar. The explanation now lives in ModeHeader, so no view can ship without one, and the grey uppercase eyebrow that restated each title in different words is gone.

3. The panel

The same object was hand-built four different ways: Card vs raw section, --r vs --r-lg, --line vs --line-strong, filled header vs no header. One <Panel> now owns all four decisions, so a fifth variant cannot appear by accident.

Note the vertical gain. Dropping the eyebrow line returns roughly 18px per panel, on a page that stacks four of them.

4. Selection

"Selected" rendered five ways: a gold underline on nav, a cyan tint on tabs, a soft fill on a picked team row, a soft fill plus inset bar on a picked region row, and a different mix plus bar on a tier-highlighted row. One treatment now covers all of them.

Defined once in base.css as --selected-bg, --selected-line and --selected-bar. --rank-gold now means rank quality only: podium, S tier, confidence.

5. Pagination

Three shapes: the team board, the match ledger with its rows-per-page select stranded up in the filter card, and the drawer's copy of the board markup with a pile of size overrides. One <Pager> with a density prop now serves all three, and it shows both the row range and the page number instead of one or the other.

6. Empty and error states

DataState already existed and was good, but it had no slot for a retry button, so every caller that needed one hand-built its own state instead. Adding an action slot removed three bespoke copies.

7. Region badges

Six regions rendered as photographic league logos and three as hand-drawn line-art glyphs, in the same column. The frame is now identical for every region and the line-art motifs are gone: contents differ only as logo or code.

8. The rail

The rail stacked seven summary surfaces next to a table that is already the summary, each with its own grey subtitle line. It is now three panels. The tier strip stays in the rail.

Corrected after review. The first pass moved the tier strip into the board panel. That was wrong twice over: it is not where you expect it, and the cells were sized in proportion to team count, which works in a narrow rail but falls apart at full board width. With a real distribution of S=2, A=3, B=32, C=16, the B cell swallowed most of the row and left a large gap before C. Cells are now an even grid.

12. One deviation from the review

The published review proposed --t-6: 1.35rem. It shipped as 1.25rem: six existing sizes (1.15, 1.2, 1.32, 1.35, 1.36, 1.4) collapse onto that step within about 10% each, where 1.35 would have pushed the 1.15rem tier-count figures up by nearly 18%.

The review also proposed dropping the league logos in favour of a code badge everywhere. That was not done: the logos are the strongest recognition cue in an esports product. The inconsistency was the hand-drawn line-art glyphs sitting beside photographs, so the frame was unified and the glyphs removed instead. Regions without a logo show their code in the same frame.
