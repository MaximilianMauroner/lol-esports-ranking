# L7: Tournament schedule

## Sub-features

Competition selection, schedule, confirmed winners, live leads, bracket tracing, and refresh.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose Tournaments in Primary navigation, or open `/#tournaments`.

## Driving it with T3 preview

Select the native `#tournament-event` control. Click `Schedule` and inspect sections `live`, `upcoming`, and `results`, and `unresolved` when present. Click `Bracket`, select a team, and check its highlighted trace. For completed series, check the confirmed `Winner:` label and winner emphasis. A live lead must not show a confirmed winner. Click `Refresh` and compare results.

## Gotchas

Forecast and ledger paths require `VITE_TOURNAMENT_FORECASTS_ENABLED=1` plus their matching artifacts. Use an owned local feed for offline work. Without it the dev server can collect the live Leaguepedia feed. Missing stages or series fail their specific journeys.
