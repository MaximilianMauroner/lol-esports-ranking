## Gotchas

The manual preview does not require Power ratings or the forecast feature flag. Selecting the full field alone must not request ratings or add percentages. Stats load ratings only after the user requests them. Once requested, edits recompute until the user cancels or leaves the preview.

The stats model samples uniform legal matchings. It does not reproduce Riot's official draw probabilities. The official-source Worlds simulation still rejects unavailable draw probabilities. The intervals measure sampling error, not model confidence. A sampled zero or one is not a certain outcome.

A named Play-In team needs that participant in the local feed and a unique canonical rating match. Live teams get missing regions from their rating standings. A fixture without qualified entrants, a required draw, or ratings fails the corresponding journey. Use the synthetic fixture recipes in `tests/fixtures/swissPreviewFixtures.ts`. Do not fetch providers to fill a fixture gap.
