Draws, winners, record buckets, scenarios, shared addresses, and live locks.

Choose Tournaments, select Worlds 2026 Main Event, then click `Swiss preview`.

## Gotchas

Play-In has no Swiss preview. The manual preview does not require Power ratings or the forecast feature flag. Selecting the full field must not add advancement percentages. A named Play-In team still needs that participant in the local feed. A fixture without qualified entrants or the required draw fails the corresponding journey.
