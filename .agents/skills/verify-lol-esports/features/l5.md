# L5: Regions

## Sub-features

Regional strength, strongest teams, depth, and scope changes.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose Regions in Primary navigation, or open `/#regions`.

## Driving it with T3 preview

Require heading `Region power`. Click a button titled `Open LCK region detail` when LCK is available. Inspect `LCK summary`, `LCK teams`, and `LCK match and score history`. Close the dialog before using the `Season` select. Change the ranking period and confirm that the displayed scope and region history change together. Use comparison checkboxes to exercise the region comparison entry. The `Region power over time` chart follows these checkboxes. Hover, focus and use arrow keys, or scrub with touch to inspect a day and the contributing teams. Check the daily history at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px. The chart must state that it uses the selected snapshot's eligible field, carries values forward between updates, and can have fewer than three contributors. It must not claim to reconstruct eligibility on past dates.

## Gotchas

Top-three strength and region depth are different measures. Do not treat raw team counts as league strength.
