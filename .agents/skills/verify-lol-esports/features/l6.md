# L6: Match history and event groups

## Sub-features

Event groups, league and year filters, series details, and pagination.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Choose Matches in Primary navigation, or open `/#matches`.

## Driving it with T3 preview

Require the page heading `Match history`, the panel heading `Series ledger`, and region `Match history results`. Use native selects `League`, `Event`, and `Match history year` when present. Expand a series from the current snapshot, use `[aria-label="Match history pagination"]` and the `Next page` button to paginate, save its address, reload, and use Back. Compare the rows and filters.
