# L4: Comparisons

## Sub-features

Team comparison, region comparison, removal, analysis, and head-to-head.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Use the `Compare` checkboxes on the Rankings board or Regions view.

## Driving it with T3 preview

Select two checkboxes with accessible names `Compare ` plus the entity label. Click `Open comparison`. Require the `Team comparison` dialog for teams. Remove one with `Remove ` plus its name plus ` from comparison`. Close the dialog and use `Clear` in the compare region.

| ID | Public actions and expected result | Source / regression references | Reset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L4 | Add teams or regions up to the displayed limit to comparison, remove one, inspect analysis and head-to-head. Labels and chart series must agree. Colors follow the current selection order and can change after removal. Claims use the same scope. | CompareDock, CompareDrawer, CompareAnalysis, HeadToHead; compare and public matchup tests | Clear comparison. |

## Gotchas

The selection limit is four. The limit message appears after four picks. Picking beyond the limit replaces the oldest choice. Missing same-scope entities fail the comparison journey.
