# Demacia Cup season lead-in

Season match ledgers and team Power charts include the previous December's
Demacia Cup as a lead-in. The games keep their source dates, event names,
rating changes, and storage year. They remain in the previous year's archive.

The lead-in does not change season records, eligibility, or rating calculations.
Those games already enter the rating model once in chronological order.
Checkpoint and event-specific scopes retain their existing boundaries.
The UI expands the source abbreviation `DCup` to `Demacia Cup`.

Demacia Cup uses the same event tier and weight as EWC: `minor-international`,
with K = 23 and weight = 23/14 (about 1.64 times a regular-season game).
It remains an LPL regional competition. Its games receive full event weight
after Worlds; the preseason discount does not apply to Demacia Cup.
